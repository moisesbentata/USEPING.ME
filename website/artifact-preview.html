FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Ping lives entirely inside WhatsApp, an app you already have. There's nothing else to install.

Is my data private?

Yes — your conversations and memories are yours. Ping only remembers what you choose to tell it, and you can ask it to forget anything, any time.

Can I cancel any time?

Yep — just tell Ping in the chat that you want to cancel. No forms, no phone calls, no "are you sure" dark patterns.

What can Ping actually do today?

Remember facts about your life and recall them by meaning, set one-off and recurring reminders, plan smart lead-up check-ins for big events, remind other people on your behalf, understand voice notes, and search the live web for current information. More is being added constantly.

Which countries does it work in?

Anywhere WhatsApp works. Message us if you hit an issue with your number.
